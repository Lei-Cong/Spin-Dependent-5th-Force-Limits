### 2. Corrected p–N constraints associated with Jackson Kimball et al. (2017)

**Numerical correction (confirmed by Wenjing Zhou and Derek F. Jackson Kimball):**
The relevant p–N constraint values extracted from
[Jackson Kimball et al., Phys. Rev. D 96, 075004 (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.96.075004)
should be multiplied by a factor of \(1/2\).

The same constraint data were also used in Safronova et al. (2018), but the
interaction formula adopted there differs from that in the 2017 primary
publication. For these datasets, the convention and formula in the original
2017 publication are used.

Affected files:

- `910Safronova_2018_Kimball_m_abs_pN.csv`
- `910Safronova_2018_Youdin_m_abs_pN.csv`
