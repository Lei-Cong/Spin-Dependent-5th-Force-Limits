Future direction · AI for science

Toward an AI-ready scientific system.

SD5thF is building the trusted data foundation needed for a future in which scientific knowledge, computation, and experiments can work together more effectively.

Our vision

Connect knowledge across the research cycle.

Our long-term goal is to connect curated scientific data, primary literature, physical definitions, AI-assisted reasoning, high-performance computing, and experimental workflows.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Navigate the field

Trace constraints from numerical curves to interaction models, experimental methods, and primary publications.

02 / DISCOVER

Identify open space

Reveal gaps in parameter space, metadata, experimental coverage, and connections between research communities.

03 / DEVELOP

Support new ideas

Help researchers develop hypotheses and compare possible experimental strategies using well-structured evidence.

04 / ANALYZE

Connect results

Make new measurements easier to compare, interpret, reproduce, and incorporate into the broader research landscape.

What we are building now

A reliable foundation before automation.

The immediate priority is a transparent, citable, machine-readable database with consistent conventions, literature provenance, experimental metadata, and stable software interfaces. These foundations are essential before AI systems can responsibly assist with scientific interpretation or experimental design.

  1. Curated dataStandardized limits with clear physical conventions.
  2. Linked evidenceConnections among curves, publications, methods, sources, and sensors.
  3. Open interfacesVersioned records that people and research software can reuse.
  4. Scientific oversightExpert review, traceability, and clear treatment of uncertainty.
